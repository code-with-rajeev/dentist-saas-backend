
Dentist AI Based SAAS Tool

TechStack: FastAPI, Uvicorn[standard], python-dotenv , [NOT FOR MVP: Celery, Redis, LangChain]

Role Dentist (DOCTOR/DENTIST, staff: ['assistant', 'receptionist', 'helper', 'compounder', other])

Authorization and Role based access (Clinic-Based-Seperation):
TOP: Clinic Owner/Dentist
- can book/cancel/confirm appointment
- add/edit follow-up
- can prescribe meds, patient details
- can't modify after submission
- can't access outside clinic level, and can't access system level config
- can add staff, edit permission
- can edit limited allowed clinic level settings only (EXAMPLE: (timing=allowed, name=not allowed))

BOTTOM: Staff level (assistant, helper etc)
- limited clinic level permission, allowed by clinic owner
